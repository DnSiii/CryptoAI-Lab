## Fixed decision rules (no optimization after PnL)
At hour t close, using only fully closed bars through t, calculate hourly log returns for BTC and ETH and the cross-sectional spread s_t = r_ETH,t - r_BTC,t. Use trailing 168 fully closed hours, excluding t, to estimate spread median and MAD; robust scale = 1.4826*MAD. If fewer than 168 prior observations or scale <= 0, target cash. Define z_t=(s_t-median)/scale. When |z_t|>=3, open at **t+1 open** a dollar-neutral BTC/ETH pair opposite the observed ETH-versus-BTC spread, with gross notional 0.50 NAV (0.25 each). Hold for exactly 6 hours then close at the next available open; do not overlap positions, do not pyramid. No entries when |z|<3. Signal and execution must be separated by at least one open; reject data gaps, zero or invalid prices. No C1 momentum, allocator, 3x2 router, cash-lock governor, or funding-only signal is reused. Position sizing remains constant gross, with no leverage escalation. This design does not assume shorting is available on spot: if native perp execution is unavailable, the candidate is non-executable and must be rejected, not silently converted.
