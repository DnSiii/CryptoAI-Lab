**Reconciliation:** for OPEN, independently compute the per-leg fee as min(native lots * dated per-lot entry rate, cap fraction * native lots * original per-lot traded premium), for the short and long legs separately. The scenario's journal fee must be at least the dated native amount and at least its fixed severe/supersevere fee floor. Slippage is separately charged and never substituted for fees. This is lower-bound consistency only, not exact fill proof, because the scenario fee may exceed the historical fee.

**Independent verification:** separate arithmetic reexecution with no import from the production calculator; adversarial cases include overlapping dates, unsigned/invalid bundle, insufficient fee, changed original premium, cap, tier and lot mismatch. Tests use synthetic data only and may never produce original economic certification.
