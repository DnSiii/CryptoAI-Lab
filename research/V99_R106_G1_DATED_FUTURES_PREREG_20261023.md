# V99 R106 G1 — dated-futures convergence, preregistered source gate (2026-10-23)

**New mechanism, not a G1 backtest or a promotion.** C1/C2 directional, D1 perpetual-funding carry, E1 passive spot and F1 perpetual-basis convergence are permanently rejected. This proposal is economically distinct: buy spot BTC/ETH and short an *expiring, USDT-linear quarterly future* at a positive basis, then converge at contractual settlement. Unlike perpetual swaps, a dated future has an expiry and does not depend on 8-hour perpetual funding. Unlike F1, convergence is anchored by a contractual delivery rather than speculative perp mean reversion. No short naked directional beta is allowed.

## Before any G1 historical return or signal calculation

- Fixed TRAIN only [2021-12-09 00:00 UTC, 2023-10-01 00:00 UTC), five unchanged R106 chronological folds; sealed validation [2023-10-01, 2024-01-18) and final holdout >=2024-01-18 not queried.
- Fixed eligible instrument family: USDT-linear BTC-USDT and ETH-USDT quarterly FUTURES expiring 2022-03-25, 2022-06-24, 2022-09-30, 2022-12-30, 2023-03-31, 2023-06-30, 2023-09-29, no substitution by inverse/coin-margined or perpetual contracts. Instrument existence, exact listing and expiry and historical delivery settlement must be proven by original exchange receipts, not inferred from name alone.
- Source gate probes exactly 2022-03-25, 2022-12-30 and 2023-09-29 contracts for BTC and ETH with the first-party OKX history-candles endpoint. Each query requests the 1h candle history strictly before expiry. These six checks test early/mid/late TRAIN *data availability only*. They do not select a winning expiry or optimize a strategy. If any sample cannot be retrieved, fail closed and report which contract/endpoint, not an economic rejection. Do not change dates or assets to chase a pass.
- If and only if first-party expired-contract history is retrievable, collect complete hourly FUTURES contract prices for all seven expiries, original instrument metadata and contractual delivery/settlement, and join the previously authenticated 15,864-hour OKX spot TRAIN. Independently verify all raw pages, hashes, time grid, missing listings and settlement dates. Do not infer unseen prices or fill missing candles.
- **Fixed prospective G1 economic rules, before PnL**: at 00:00 UTC each Monday, for each asset independently, select the earliest *prelisted* eligible quarterly expiry with >=30 and <=110 days remaining; only enter a fully matched spot-long/dated-future-short pair when previous completed hourly close-based annualized premium exceeds **10%** and gross positive premium exceeds the fixed severe four-side roundtrip cost **0.48%** plus a 0.25% margin reserve. No intra-week entry, no parameter search, no averaging down. Exit at contractual delivery, or if maintenance margin would be breached; otherwise hold. At most 25% starting NAV per asset committed, remaining cash as collateral/reserve. USDT-linear contract size, lot granularity, margin and mark-price liquidation must be obtained from exchange metadata; do not silently assume linearity. If metadata unavailable, model explicit conservative stress and block deployment.
- Two fixed execution scenarios: severe 0.12% and supersevere 0.24% **per executed side**, including assumed fee and slippage, applied on each spot and futures open/close; additionally 1% and 2% per-side adverse fill shocks. Futures funding = zero by contract type, but borrow/collateral interest, margin liquidation and settlement cash must be explicitly modeled; unknown values are not set to zero as a profit benefit. Strict t-1 information, complete cash + position + margin journal, original market opens only as hypothetical fill reference. No real historical fills or certified account profits.
- Precommitted full TRAIN gates if data complete: positive compounded modeled ROI in both costs, >=4/5 severe and >=3/5 supersevere positive chronological folds, both max DD >=-35%, both daily PF >1, both best-day-removed ROI >0, worst day >=-15%, no isolated tail concentration, supersevere > same-cost fully collateralized passive spot/cash benchmark by 2pp, valid 3x2 regimes, original contract delivery/fees/settlement traceability, independent per-hour accounting oracle and adverse shocks. Any fail -> permanent economic rejection, no retune. Passing simulation alone is provisional and **cannot** unlock sealed validation until freeze and source/provenance gates.
- The source gate **does not compute basis, PnL or strategy outcomes**. Missing historical account-specific fees/fills are not secretly represented as real. Historical hypothetical fills can only be modeled. No orders, Frozen, legacy champion, paper/results or public dashboard promotion.
