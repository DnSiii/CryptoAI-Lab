## Immutable replay rules
- Separate BTC and ETH coin wallets; no cross-asset coin netting or synthetic FX conversion. Each event is chronological, carries a unique position identifier, and occurs entirely inside TRAIN [2021-12-09,2023-10-01); option expiry also strictly inside TRAIN.
- Native quantity, original venue bid/ask and displayed size, native fee, and initial/maintenance collateral are explicit event inputs, **never** inferred from source units or the USD-equivalent legacy model. Historical provenance must be separately authenticated before using any non-fixture result. All model inputs must be strictly known before the event; no future quote or lookahead. Two-leg timestamps on both venue and arrival clocks must differ <=1s.
- At opening, short-put bid minus long-put ask produces gross coin premium; both leg fees and conservative slippage are explicit nonnegative coin debits. Reserve a separately supplied positive initial coin margin; enforce cash >= reserved margin, with no reuse. Mark-to-exit liabilities use short ask minus long bid, plus both exit costs, and original synchronized quotes. Unrealized liabilities are subtracted from wallet NAV; cash is not NAV. The ledger never treats an as-of mark as an actual fill.
- At expiry, use separately supplied original settlement index and native inverse payoff: quantity * (max(K_short-index,0)-max(K_long-index,0)) / index. Explicit delivery fees are coin debits. No optional/missing settlement index or fees. Enforce expiry chronology and no overdue open positions.
- Maintenance check must compare NAV against supplied maintenance requirements and **fail closed** if breached; do not silently assume liquidation fill. Scenario-specific paths must be replayed separately. Never label historical account margin as certified without dated first-party terms and account evidence.
- Independent oracle will recompute coin wallet, reserved margin, open/mark/settlement balances without importing the production accounting function. Adversarial controls for fabricated premium, reused collateral, stale marks, noncausal quotes, settlement FX, missing margin and forged NAV must fail.
- Only five chronological TRAIN folds, 3x2 regimes, severe/supersevere, full lifecycle original source and independent gates may qualify a *later* integrated economic result. **No validation >=2023-10-01 or final holdout**, Frozen, paper, champion, public performance mutation or orders.
