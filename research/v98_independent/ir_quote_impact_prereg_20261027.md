## Single seven-slot portfolio
Let x_i=log(C_i/C_i,prev)-signed funding event. Let q_i be native hourly quote. For each asset on last 672h, define hourly scaled price impact z_i=|x_i|/sqrt(max(q_i,1)/mean(q_i)); impact I_i=mean(z_i) (floor 1e-6); signed impact-resilience A_i=sum(x_i/sqrt(max(q_i,1)/mean(q_i)))/(sum(|x_i|/sqrt(max(q_i,1)/mean(q_i)))+1e-8) in [-1,1]. Liquidity renewal L_i=mean(q_i last 168h)/mean(q_i first 504h), clipped [0.25,4]. Net trend T_i=sum x_i last 336h; downside pressure H_i=sum(max(-x_i,0)/sqrt(max(q_i,1)/mean(q_i))) over last 168h. Market impact regime score Z=median_i(A_i) and market liquidity renewal U=median_i(L_i). This uses continuous volume-scaled impact and quote renewal, **not** synchronized hourly breadth, candle wicks, extreme jump selection or IO inventory holding.
1. **Growth Core:** long-only all four assets, weights proportional sqrt(mean q_i)/(I_i+1e-6) times max(.1,1+T_i), normalized. Exposure can compound without arbitrary CAGR ceiling.
2. **Opportunity Sleeve:** long 100% asset maximizing A_i*L_i (deterministic ties), always financed when source valid. Hypothesis: renewed-liquidity positive absorption predicts stronger upside.
3. **Relative-Value Sleeve:** long 50% asset maximizing A_i/(I_i+1e-6), short 50% asset minimizing same score; distinct extrema by deterministic ordering. Always financed when valid. Hypothesis: resilience dispersion creates market-relative spread.
4. **Stress/Recovery Sleeve:** if Z<0, short 100% asset maximizing H_i/(I_i+1e-6); otherwise long 100% asset maximizing T_i*L_i/(I_i+1e-6). Always financed when valid.
5. **Regime Router:** STRESS if Z<0 and U<1; RECOVERY if Z>=0 and U>=1; NORMAL otherwise.
6. **Dynamic Allocator:** Core/Opp/RV/Stress fixed budgets STRESS [.63,.12,.10,.15], RECOVERY [.57,.19,.12,.12], NORMAL [.62,.16,.12,.10]. Inactive/ablated optional budgets transfer fully to Core. Target gross STRESS .78, RECOVERY .92, NORMAL .88, never >1, no hidden leverage.
