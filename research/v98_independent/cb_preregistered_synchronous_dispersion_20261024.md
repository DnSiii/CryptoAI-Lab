## Seven fixed slots

1. **Growth Core:** at each 336h epoch, long up to two positive-1008h total-return (log-price minus sum of native signed funding) assets ranked by this net drift divided by max(336h RMS hourly log return, 0.005); weight inverse RMS volatility, else cash.
2. **Opportunity:** long one asset with the highest positive 72h log-return acceleration (last 72h minus preceding 72h), provided 336h price log return >0 and 72h acceleration >0; otherwise cash. No volume-breakout trigger.
3. **Relative Value:** dollar-neutral long top, short bottom of four 336h log price returns *minus their 336h realized signed native funding*, only if score dispersion >0.04; 0.5 long/0.5 short. No 336h funding-spread-only trigger.
4. **Stress/Recovery:** activate if median 336h hourly RMS volatility exceeds median 1008h hourly RMS volatility. If median 72h log return <0, short the weakest 72h asset; otherwise long strongest 72h asset. Otherwise cash. This is volatility-regime participation, not a post-hoc 2023/24 crash date selection.
5. **Regime Router:** BULL when >=3 assets have positive 336h log return; STRESS when cross-asset median 168h log return <0; else MIXED. STRESS takes precedence.
6. **Dynamic Allocator:** segregated initial capital fractions [0.70,0.12,0.10,0.08]. Gross within-book targets by BULL/MIXED/STRESS: Core [1.40,1.00,0.60]; Opportunity [1.20,0.80,0.30]; RV [1.00,0.80,0.70]; Stress [0.50,0.70,1.30]. Epoch-frozen targets, no NAV feedback until next epoch, no cross-book transfers.
7. **Risk Governor:** original V98 Independent 4-book independent collateral, physical prior-hour quote cap and 336h minimum rolling 24h exit capacity, per-book 25% gross capacity, 24h adverse staged liquidation, native signed funding reserve, actual t+1 open, 81-corner intrabar, three original cost tiers and both funding orderings.
