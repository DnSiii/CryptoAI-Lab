## Seven locked slots (before native source execution)
1. **Growth Core:** every 360 hours, compute for each asset the proportion of positive trailing 48-hour returns sampled hourly over the last 720 hours (673 overlapping observations), eligible only if 720-hour log return is positive and persistence >0.55. Long the highest persistence eligible at 70%, second at 30%, or single eligible at 100%, otherwise cash. The objective is sustained compounding, not minimizing volatility.
2. **Opportunity Sleeve:** every 24 hours, consider BNB/SOL with completed close above the *preceding* 144-hour high, completed trailing 24-hour quote volume >1.2 times the median of preceding 20 disjoint 24-hour volume totals, and positive 240-hour return. Long the qualifying asset with the largest 24h volume/median ratio, otherwise cash.
3. **Relative-Value Sleeve:** every 72 hours, calculate each asset's 96h and 240h excess log return over equal-weight Clean-4. Long the highest 96h excess asset only if its 96h excess >0, 240h excess >0, and its own 240h absolute log return >0. **Long-only conditional relative strength**, no dollar-neutral short and no permanent SOL short; otherwise cash. This is cross-sectional selection, not funding-spread carry or conditional downside beta.
4. **Stress/Recovery Sleeve:** every 24h, when >=3 of 4 assets have negative 72h log returns and mean 12h log return >0, long the strongest positive-12h return asset among BTC/ETH; otherwise cash. It monetizes *recovery* rather than paying continuous short hedge drag.
5. **Regime Router:** based on count of assets with positive 240h log return: RISK_ON >=3, RISK_OFF <=1, otherwise MIXED.
6. **Dynamic Allocator:** fixed, precommitted per-regime module gross target budgets [Core,Opportunity,RV,Stress]: RISK_ON [1.65,0.45,0.40,0.25], MIXED [1.25,0.40,0.50,0.40], RISK_OFF [0.75,0.25,0.25,0.60]. Shared collateral, no hidden leverage. Units persist between module updates.
7. **Risk Governor:** pre-existing CG buffered conservative physical authorization (60% post-shock NAV reserve, 40% floor, 18% maintenance, 0.25% participation, 25% staged exits, 81 OHLC corners, 24h continuation, native signed funding and mark bounds).
