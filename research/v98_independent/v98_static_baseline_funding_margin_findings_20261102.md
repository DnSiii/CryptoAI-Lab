## Independent root-cause proof

The separate closed-form forensic independently reconciled **all 36 cells**, including all **24 funding-driven gross-limit failures** and all 12 cash/BNB completed terminals. The separate hour-by-hour cash/funding oracle independently reconciled cash-only full history and the equal-weight history up to its failure, with deliberate cash tampering detected.

For a static long-only position with no intermediate rebalance, the available gross buffer equals initial uninvested capital minus entry fee plus cumulative signed funding. Changes in market price move gross notional and equity by the same amount; they cannot consume this buffer by themselves. **All 24 observed first gross violations occurred precisely at 8-hour funding settlements** and were independently reconstructed as a negative funding payment crossing the buffer. For example, base equal-weight had **+7.51** remaining immediately before the event and paid **-29.32** funding, leaving **-21.81** buffer; its cumulative signed funding had reached **-2,013.81** against an initial ~2,000 cash reserve. BTC base crossed after cumulative signed funding **-1,998.64**. This is a reproducible **conservative-envelope simulation finding**, not verified exact exchange settlement.
