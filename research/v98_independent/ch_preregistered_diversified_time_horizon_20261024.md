## Seven slots, parameters locked before economics
1. **Growth Core:** every 336h, compute each asset's 336h log return divided by realized 336h hourly log-return standard deviation times sqrt(336), floor vol 0.02. Select up to two **positive 336h** assets with highest normalized trend. Allocate 65/35 if two, 100% if one, otherwise cash. No funding in selection score; funding is charged natively in ledger.
2. **Opportunity Sleeve:** every 168h, BNB/SOL only; choose the higher **48h log-return** asset among those with positive 672h return. Long that asset, otherwise cash. This tests short-horizon continuation conditional on long-horizon positive trend, not CG breakout.
3. **Relative-Value Sleeve:** every 168h, compute BTC/ETH 168h relative log-return. **Contrarian**: long the 168h relative laggard, short the leader, each at 50% gross; if exactly tied, flat. No absolute spread threshold; distinct from CG trend pair.
4. **Stress/Recovery Sleeve:** every 168h, compute BTC 168h and 672h log returns. If 168h BTC negative, short BTC. If 168h BTC positive AND 672h BTC negative, long BTC as recovery. Otherwise flat. This tests defensive trend plus early recovery, not CG breadth-gated bear.
5. **Regime Router:** every 168h, count positive 336h trends among four assets; >=3 RISK_ON, <=1 RISK_OFF, otherwise MIXED.
6. **Dynamic Allocator:** frozen module gross budgets by router [core, opportunity, RV, stress]: RISK_ON [1.60,0.60,0.30,0.10]; MIXED [1.25,0.45,0.35,0.35]; RISK_OFF [0.45,0.15,0.40,0.70]. Hold each module's units between its own scheduled updates. No invisible resizing of other modules.
7. **Risk Governor:** reuse generic physical BA capacity and existing 60% stressed post-fill NAV reserve (CG buffered governor as **infrastructure only**, never CG alpha), signed native funding reserve, 81 OHLC corners, 24h adverse continuation, 40% NAV floor, 18% maintenance, previous-hour 0.25% participation and staged 25% exit, original fee tiers.
