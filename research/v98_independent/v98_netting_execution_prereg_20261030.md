# V98 Independent — preregistered netted one-way execution contract (2026-10-30)

**INFRASTRUCTURE ONLY; NOT A NEW STRATEGY, NOT PnL OR APPROVAL.** Original NA/NB/NC and all earlier rejections stay frozen. This document precedes the first synthetic test of a *new* V98 execution primitive. It must not be applied retrospectively to change rejected candidate receipts.

**Economic/physical problem:** prior V98 seven-slot runners charge turnover on the sum of *internal sleeve* quantity changes. On a Binance USD-M account operating in **one-way position mode**, the exchange executes only the net per-symbol delta, so two opposite virtual sleeve changes can cancel without a market order. A netted implementation can reduce unnecessary costs without changing historical price signals, inventing leverage, or fitting parameters. In **hedge mode**, the netting assumption is not automatically valid: require an account-mode attestation before claiming exchange-native fidelity. If account mode is unknown, report this as a conditional model and retain conservative non-netted costs in sensitivity.

**Frozen mechanical contract:** four virtual sleeves x four symbols remain independently tracked for ablation and attribution; the actual market inventory is the per-symbol sum. Given completed-close t signals, next-open t+1 known NAV and open prices, construct virtual targets from preregistered weights and scale all virtual sleeves uniformly if required. Charge fees/slippage **only on net executed per-symbol delta**, not virtual reallocations. Enforce virtual gross <=.84 NAV at target and <=.90 NAV post-cost/event reserve, net gross <=.90 NAV after costs/funding, no pre-existing >1.0 gross, close-before-open at the *net* order level, positive intermediate/intrabar NAV, reject zero-volume fills. Fixed ex-ante 3% event funding-loss reserve uses only calendar, inventory, open prices, NAV; actual signed rates/marks enter **ex-post accounting only**. Both carried-before-fill and new-after-fill orderings; no future mark/rate for sizing.

**Synthetic independent requirements before real PnL:** (1) offsetting internal sleeve transfers produce exactly zero exchange turnover/fees; (2) genuine net deltas incur exact fees/slippage; (3) virtual gross remains bounded even if net inventory cancels; (4) atomic net close-first ordering; (5) event reserve and signed settlement cannot use future rates to size; (6) tampered or zero-volume fills rejected; (7) deterministic invariants on randomized 4x4 virtual portfolios. Synthetic PASS is *not* a live order or economic approval.
