## Distinct economic thesis and falsification
PJ tests whether a *newly broken trailing price channel with contemporaneous traded notional expansion* produces sustained trend growth, whether cross-sectional *residual drift after removing BTC beta* adds market-neutral incremental wealth, and whether *persistent downside channel breaks* yield convex stress protection that compounds rather than merely reduces drawdown. These are not OI-positioning elasticity, funding crowding, or breadth mean reversion. Each optional sleeve must have positive net marginal log growth versus a complete-system leave-one-out counterfactual, including severe costs; zero activity is a failure, not a success. No adaptive optimization on realized PnL.

Define p_j=ln(C_j), ret_j(k)=p_j[t]-p_j[t-k]; previous 168h high Hprev=max(H[t-168:t]), previous low Lprev=min(L[t-168:t]) (exclude t); current 24h traded quote-volume V24=sum(Q[t-23:t+1]), previous 168h average 24h volume Vprev=sum(Q[t-167:t-23])/6, both positive. For beta use 336 completed hourly log returns of asset and BTC, beta=cov(r_j,r_BTC)/max(var(r_BTC),1e-8) clipped [0,2], residual=ret_j(168)-beta*ret_BTC(168). All ties by asset index. Do not inspect current bar's future high/low.

## Complete seven-slot system
1. **Growth Core**: trailing 168h channel break with volume >1.15*Vprev and ret(336) direction agreement. If C[t]>Hprev and ret(336)>0, go long; if C[t]<Lprev and ret(336)<0, go short. Rank eligible by abs(ret(336))/max(std(hourly returns[t-335:t+1])*sqrt(336),.04), top 2. Weights 0.42 and 0.24 with sign. Otherwise cash. Max core gross .66.
2. **Opportunity Sleeve**: BTC channel-break impulse spillover. If BTC ret(24)>.045 and BTC V24>1.4*Vprev, select the non-BTC asset with highest positive ret(24) among assets whose ret(72)>0, go +.22; if BTC ret(24)<-.045 and same volume test, select non-BTC most negative ret(24) with ret(72)<0, go -.22. No eligible -> cash.
3. **Relative Value**: beta-neutral residual drift. Rank non-BTC residuals over 168h, top and bottom; require residual spread>.12, top residual>+.04, bottom residual<-.04, and 24h returns preserve residual ordering. Long top +.13, short bottom -.13. This is dollar-neutral, not guaranteed beta-neutral; report beta mismatch as diagnostic.
4. **Stress/Recovery**: when BTC ret(72)<-.09, BTC close below trailing 336h low (exclude t), and 24h volume>1.3*Vprev, short BTC -.20. Conversely, after BTC ret(336)<-.18 and BTC ret(24)>.065 with volume>1.3*Vprev, long BTC +.20. Otherwise cash. Stress trades only on independent tail conditions.
5. **Regime Router**: 'breakout' when >=2 assets have channel breaks with 336h agreement; 'shock' when BTC ret(72)<-.09; otherwise 'range'. Priority breakout=(Opportunity,RV,Stress), shock=(Stress,RV,Opportunity), range=(RV,Opportunity,Stress).
6. **Dynamic Allocator**: core preserved; optional budget min(.30, .84-core gross); sequential priority, calculate 168h completed-hourly return correlation between candidate sleeve and allocated aggregate. If corr>.45, scale candidate gross by 0.5, otherwise full; clip to budget without gross renormalization. Each ablation recomputes allocation independently.
7. **Risk Governor**: decision gross <=.84, core <=.66, optional <=.30, original atomic one-way fill and hourly open-gap/intrabar/funding/NAV checks. Fees/slippage base 4/2 bps, severe 10/10, supersevere 15/20. No hidden leverage.
