# V98 Independent — NORTH STAR

Status: ACTIVE PROJECT OBJECTIVE.

## Mission

V98 is not a single alpha experiment and is not a conservative replacement for older engines.

V98 exists to become the strongest trading engine in the project: maximize long-run compounded wealth with no artificial upside ceiling, while preserving causal execution, survivability, reproducibility and robustness.

The ambition is explicit: V98 must ultimately prove that it is materially superior to every prior engine, including V14, V15 and V99. "Almost as good" is not success.

## Independence rule

V14/V15/V99 are external benchmarks only. Their parameters, signals, architecture and detailed performance must not be used to tune or select V98 components.

V98 is built from V98-only evidence. External engines are consulted only after a V98 candidate is frozen and has passed its own training + validation process. That preserves an honest superiority test.

## Design consequence

Stop treating V98 as "find one tiny strategy that is perfect alone".

V98 is a composed engine with independent modules:

1. Growth Core — persistent directional/convex return engine.
2. Opportunity Sleeve — captures rare high-upside dislocations/trends.
3. Relative-Value Sleeve — market-neutral alpha.
4. Stress/Recovery Sleeve — crash/reversal/defensive alpha.
5. Regime Router — decides which modules are eligible and how much capital they receive.
6. Dynamic Allocator — reallocates risk using only causal evidence.
7. Risk Governor — controls ruin, concentration, tails and gross exposure without imposing a return ceiling.

A module may enter the engine only through preregistered V98-only evidence. Rejected experiments stay rejected; they may inform what not to do, but cannot be cosmetically revived.

## Optimization target

Primary objective: terminal compounded wealth / long-run CAGR.

No arbitrary return target or ceiling.

Risk is a constraint and diagnostic, not the objective to minimize. The engine may take meaningful risk when the evidence supports it, but it must avoid ruin, hidden leverage, pathological concentration and non-causal execution.

The final V98 should seek convexity: participate aggressively when expected edge is unusually strong, remain adaptive across regimes, and reduce exposure when expected edge deteriorates.

## Final proof

A final frozen V98 must survive:
- chronological training folds;
- realistic costs and funding;
- severe and supersevere stress;
- regime diagnostics;
- concentration/tails;
- reproducibility and causality audits;
- separate validation;
- a genuinely untouched forward holdout.

Only after that may V14/V15/V99 be opened as external benchmarks.

Final success means V98 demonstrates clear superiority as a complete engine, not merely a statistically acceptable isolated alpha.

The ceiling is intentionally undefined.
