## Phase1240 Regime Router — close t, execute no earlier than t+1 open
Use exactly 96 completed hourly log returns ending at t. Let m be equal-weight market hourly log return, cross-sectional dispersion D be standard deviation across four 96h cumulative asset log returns, downside skew K be third centered moment of 96 market returns divided by sigma^3 (floor 1e-4), M24=sum of last 24 market returns. Native funding breadth B is fraction of positive nonzero native funding observations within the same 96 completed hours (zero if no events). Three states: HAZARD if M24 < -0.08 AND K < -0.5; else DISPERSION if D > 0.06 and |sum(m)| < 0.06; else TREND. B is recorded as a causal covariate, not used to rescue a failed classifier. Finite/shape/date validation, no future-bar dependence.

## Phase1241 Dynamic Allocator — online net log growth, four sleeves

Funding sign must match Phase1234 ledger. Use predeclared conservative base paper roundtrip fee 0.001 per unit of absolute weight change, not an unpriced frictionless selector. No use of current bar's signal in its own return. Require 168 observations before learning; before that deterministic priors (TREND: [0.55,0.20,0.15,0.10], DISPERSION: [0.30,0.20,0.40,0.10], HAZARD: [0.25,0.10,0.15,0.50]). Once mature, estimate mean shrunk by 0.5 and covariance shrunk 50% toward its diagonal, variance ridge 1e-6. Optimize expected net one-hour log wealth under nonnegative sleeve weights summing to 1, using 32 deterministic exponentiated-gradient steps with score mu - cov @ w, scaled by fixed 100. Start from regime prior. Final signed target is the weighted sum of four sleeves, with a separately recorded positive growth-sizing scalar: max(0, min(3, 0.5*positive_shrunk_mu / max(variance,1e-6))) evaluated on past paper returns. This scalar is a *numerical and survival search envelope*, not an upside target; risk governor separately approves all exposures. No post-result sign/window/fee/sizing rescue.
