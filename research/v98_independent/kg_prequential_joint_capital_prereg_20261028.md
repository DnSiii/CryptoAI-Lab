## Hypothesis and causal information set

A portfolio may grow faster if *joint capital competition* learns, from strictly previous 168h shadow expert-versus-Core outcomes, which independent payoff types deserve scarce collateral. Every expert has a fixed, nonoptimized predictive rule; shadow comparisons are **prequential**: exposure selected at t-168 is scored only on t-167..t, and never used for execution before t+1 actual open. Shadow returns are an allocation signal only, NOT reported as realized strategy PnL. Full and each ablation independently settle real positions and funding on the canonical ledger. This is a new capital auction with causal shadow feedback and an explicit cost hurdle, not transplanting any rejected engine/sleeve.

- Warmup 744h, decisions every 168h at completed hour t, first execution at actual open t+1, no same-hour close fill. All native signed funding events and conservative 81-corner physical governor remain unchanged. USD10,000 initial NAV; no borrowed cash, gross <=1, no hidden leverage.
- Net hourly log proxy: log(close[t]/close[t-1]) - native funding_rate[t] * funding_event[t] (completed events only). Shadow net is a causal feature, not a substitute for native realized settlement.
- Growth Core: rank 4 assets by `0.65*net_336h + 0.35*net_672h - 0.5*downside_semivol_336h*sqrt(168)`; long two highest-ranked, inverse semivol weighted. No asset selection from hindsight.
- Opportunity Sleeve: long asset with highest positive `(net_return_last72 - net_return_prev72)*max(log(mean_quote_volume_last72 / mean_quote_volume_prev336),0)`, only if its last72 net return is positive. No volume extrapolation. Fixed potential budget 0.18.
- Relative-Value Sleeve: long asset with most negative 72h *market-relative* return among those with positive 672h net return AND positive 24h net return; cross-sectional mean-reversion rebound, no pair short or unverified basis. Fixed potential budget 0.12.
- Stress/Recovery Sleeve: in STRESS short the asset with largest downside semivol 336h; in RECOVERY long asset with highest 24h net rebound; otherwise inactive. Fixed potential budget 0.10.
- Regime Router: STRESS if equal-weight net market last72h <-0.035; otherwise RECOVERY if last24h >0.015 and prior72h <0; otherwise NORMAL.
- Dynamic Allocator: maintain **four most recent completed** 168h prequential shadow relative-to-Core observations for each optional expert. For the prior epoch's fixed exposures, compute relative net log proxy on the subsequent 168h, subtract **0.002 times L1 turnover** between prior expert and prior Core as a fixed roundtrip cost hurdle. Missing or inactive expert has score -0.03. Initial score 0, no hindsight. Mean of available prior observations `adv` produces allocation `potential_budget * clip(0.5 + adv/0.03, 0, 1)`; if current expert inactive, budget 0. All remaining capital stays with Core; ablated sleeve's budget reverts to Core without re-optimizing surviving sleeves. This causal portfolio-wide capital auction is the architectural hypothesis, not a post-result threshold.
- Risk Governor: generic shared-collateral conservative GL authorization and canonical signed physical settlement; gross NORMAL 0.85, STRESS 0.65, RECOVERY 0.75. Base/severe/supersevere native costs, both funding orders, prior-only risk bounds. Stress tails, concentration and NAV tracked.
