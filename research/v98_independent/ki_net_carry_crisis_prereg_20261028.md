## Mechanism

Hypothesis: persistent cross-sectional **net funding dispersion** creates a portfolio financing opportunity when combined with *relative directional strength* and a trend-conditioned crisis hedge. Unlike KG's backward shadow-score auction, KI uses a fixed ex-ante risk-budget map with an independent, completed-data **carry-to-momentum agreement** filter. It can fail; no retrospective parameter rescue.

- 744 completed hours warmup. Global decision epochs every 168 completed hours, t=744,912,...; only bars/funding at <=t, trade earliest at actual open t+1. All five books independently settle native signed funding and physical fills, with two funding orders and base/severe/supersevere costs.
- Let net log return per asset be log(C_t/C_{t-1}) minus signed native funding rate when an event occurred. Let `m336`, `m168`, `m72`, `m24` be sums of that proxy over strictly completed windows, and `carry168` be sum of signed native funding rate over 168 completed hours. This proxy is for ex-ante features only, not realized PnL.
- **Growth Core:** long the two assets with largest `m336 - 0.35*downside_semivol336*sqrt(168) - 0.5*max(carry168,0)`; weight by positive softmax of scores with temperature 0.20 (normalized within selected two). Cash is allowed when both m336 <= 0: core then holds no exposure, not a fabricated cash yield.
- **Opportunity Sleeve:** 0.16 capital long the asset with highest `m72 - m168*72/168` among assets with positive `m72` and positive quote-volume acceleration `mean(q last72) / mean(q prior168) >1`; requires acceleration score positive. A true recent net momentum acceleration, not an OI/spot/trade-count/1m signal.
- **Relative-Value Sleeve:** 0.12 capital in a zero-net-notional pair: long the asset maximizing `m168 - 0.75*carry168`, short the one minimizing it, with each leg half of sleeve gross. Activate only if the long score >0, the short score <0, and their scores differ by >0.04. This is explicitly a *funding-aware relative directional spread*, not a synthetic basis arbitrage.
- **Stress/Recovery Sleeve:** 0.10 capital; STRESS if equal-weight market m168 < -0.07, then short the weakest m72 asset only if m72<0; RECOVERY if market m24 >0.02 following prior completed 72h market <0, then long strongest m24 asset; otherwise in NORMAL long the strongest asset only when its m24>0.025 and aggregate m168>0.04. This includes a distinct positive-trend convexity regime, rather than rare crisis-only activation.
- **Regime Router:** the above strictly ordered STRESS > RECOVERY > NORMAL classification. NORMAL, STRESS, RECOVERY gross budgets respectively 0.90, 0.70, 0.80.
- **Dynamic Allocator:** no shadow PnL, no fitted thresholds. Each optional sleeve is fully funded only when its above source condition is satisfied; remaining optional budget is returned to Core, but if Core is in cash it remains cash. No re-optimization after ablation. For every book, weights are `(1-sum(active budgets))*core + sum(active budget*expert)`; minus-book zeros its own budget and returns it to Core. Absolute gross <=1; all 4 books and core-only share the same independent physical governor.
