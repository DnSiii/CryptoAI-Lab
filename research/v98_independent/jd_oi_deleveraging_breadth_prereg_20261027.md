## One complete seven-slot architecture
1. **Growth Core:** long-only positive 672h funding-net trend divided by 672h downside volatility floor 0.002; inverse-downside-vol fallback. This remains the growth anchor, not a minimization-of-risk objective.
2. **Opportunity:** short-covering/deleveraging continuation, not the previously tested rising-OI leverage crowding. For each asset calculate 48h log OI contraction and 48h funding-net price return; long the asset maximizing positive product (-OI48)*positive-return48 if both are positive; otherwise no Opportunity exposure. No sign flip after observing performance.
3. **Relative Value:** long-only *low-positioning-pressure catch-up* asset: rank each asset by (48h native-net price return minus 168h native-net price return scaled by 48/168) minus 0.5 times 48h log OI change; select highest positive score, otherwise no RV. No short pair, no beta residual and no synthetic PnL.
4. **Stress/Recovery:** cross-asset drawdown breadth and forced-positioning release. STRESS when >=3 assets have negative 168h funding-net returns; RECOVERY when >=2 assets have 48h positive net returns AND median OI48 change <0; otherwise NORMAL. In STRESS allocate long to asset with lowest 168h downside semivol only when its net48 is positive; otherwise allocate to BTC (defensive inventory, not cash). In RECOVERY allocate long to asset maximizing positive net48 times positive OI contraction; if none, long BTC. In NORMAL allocate long to BTC. This module is explicitly financed and subject to full-minus-Stress marginal gate.
5. **Regime Router:** the fixed STRESS/RECOVERY/NORMAL predicates above. Gross exposure NORMAL 1.0, STRESS 0.80, RECOVERY 0.95.
6. **Dynamic Allocator:** a priori Core weight >=0.60, optional weights from clipped 168h downside-vol inverse weights. Fixed initial risk budgets Core .65, Opportunity .14, RV .11, Stress .10; multiply each optional budget by clip(core_downside_vol/sleeve_downside_vol, 0.5, 1.5), normalize optional shares to at most 0.35 and transfer inactive or ablated sleeve budget to Core. Each physical ablation independently recomputes book with the exact same prior-only signal, never removes a return stream synthetically. No estimated forward alpha, no backtest-dependent tuning, no gross >1.
7. **Risk Governor:** unchanged prior-only conservative margin/exit governor, native signed funding/mark, next-open OHLC settlement, three cost tiers and both funding orders, no hidden borrowing.
