## Implications for the next genuinely distinct architecture

- Growth, **including compounded deployable capital at US$1m/US$10m**, remains the optimization target. Physical capacity, margin and cost tails remain constraints; never declare 90/90 mechanical survival to be economic success.
- Avoid another near-duplicate of fixed-budget periodic BTC/ETH core plus short-lived SOL/BNB satellites. A new hypothesis must specify a genuinely different *economic mechanism* and account for the physical liquidity ceiling **before** looking at its returns.
- Demand native funding and execution costs, per-year and per-regime growth, ablations, severe and supersevere, concentration and tail tests. Keep all original rejections intact; no post-result sign/threshold/window rescue.
- 2025 future holdout remains sealed. V16/V99 Frozen and paper are outside scope and unchanged.

**Decision:** no V98 engine is approved. This audit is a negative-design constraint and does not constitute a new candidate, completion gate, or trading authorization.
