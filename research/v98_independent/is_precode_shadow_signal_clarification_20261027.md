# IS pre-code clarification — 2026-10-27, before source implementation or economic execution

The previous-epoch "held signal" in IS's prequential allocator is the **committed prior-epoch raw expert shadow signal**, not a claim that the real USD10k physical book held that position. The shadow signal is committed at the previous completed close and scored only on subsequent completed signed returns; it is an eligibility feature, never an additional PnL account, synthetic NAV credit, or risk exemption. Scoring continues even when a sleeve was not actually funded, avoiding permanent activation deadlock after the first epoch. The previous epoch's raw Core signal is the comparison baseline. The current epoch's newly selected raw signal cannot enter its own prequential score. A missing/zero prior optional signal makes its score ineligible. All precommitted numerical gates, budgets and other definitions remain unchanged. This clarification is written before any IS code, source-only run, or system PnL.
