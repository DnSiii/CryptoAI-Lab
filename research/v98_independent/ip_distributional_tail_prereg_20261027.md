## Seven-slot distribution-shape architecture (single fixed hypothesis; no grid)
For each asset at t, let U_i be mean of the 12 largest positive values among prior 336 x_i (zero if fewer than 12 positive), L_i be mean absolute magnitude of 12 most negative x_i (zero if fewer than 12 negative), T_i=(U_i-L_i)/(U_i+L_i+1e-8) in [-1,1]. Define P_i as fraction of prior 336 x_i >0; J_i as fraction of prior 336 x_i whose absolute value exceeds 2 times its own median absolute x_i. Let V_i=max(sqrt(mean(x_i^2)),0.0001). Define signed net 72h N72_i=sum last 72 x_i. All computed strictly prior-only.
1. **Growth Core:** long-only 4-asset compensated exposure, weight proportional sqrt(mean native quote volume in prior 336h)/V_i, normalized to 1. This is not IO equal-weight inventory.
2. **Opportunity Sleeve:** if any asset has T_i>0 and P_i>=0.48, long 100% eligible asset with highest T_i*J_i; else CASH. Hypothesis: positive jump asymmetry combined with broad upside participation generates convex compounding.
3. **Relative-Value Sleeve:** if max(T)-min(T)>=0.05, hold 50% long asset with max T and 50% short asset with min T (unit absolute gross), else CASH. Hypothesis: cross-sectional distribution-shape premium, not funding/covariance/momentum ranking.
4. **Stress/Recovery Sleeve:** when median(N72)<0, hold 100% short asset with largest L_i/V_i (negative-tail continuation protection); otherwise hold 100% long asset with largest U_i/V_i (upside-tail capture). Not based on IO's lowest drawdown, IJ semivol ratio or IL candle wicks.
5. **Regime Router:** STRESS when median(N72)<0 and median(J)>=0.18; RECOVERY when median(N72)>0 and median(T)<0; NORMAL otherwise.
6. **Dynamic Allocator:** fixed Core/Opp/RV/Stress budgets: STRESS [.58,.10,.12,.20], RECOVERY [.48,.22,.12,.18], NORMAL [.55,.18,.15,.12]. Any inactive or ablated optional budget transfers fully to Core. Requested gross STRESS .72, RECOVERY .90, NORMAL .90; never >1 or hidden leverage.
7. **Risk Governor:** unchanged GL 81-corner order-invariant buffered physical authorization, prior-only bounds, native mark/funding, fee/slippage, staged exit, two funding order conventions. Five separately financed books (Core, Full, minus each optional).
