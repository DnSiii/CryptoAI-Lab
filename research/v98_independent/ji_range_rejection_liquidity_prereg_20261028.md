## Seven slots: single immutable architectural hypothesis
1. **Growth Core**: long-only proportional to positive 336h net log return times directional efficiency (abs net 336h return / sum absolute 1h returns), divided by 336h downside semivol floor .002; if all scores zero, inverse downside semivol. This is a trend *quality* core, not JH's 504h return/semivol core.
2. **Opportunity Sleeve**: native range-breakout acceptance: last 48h close position relative to preceding 336h high-low envelope, combined with last 48h quote-volume acceleration versus prior 288h and positive last 48h net return. Long asset with highest positive score, otherwise no opportunity allocation. Confidence clip(score/.20, .25, 1) when positive.
3. **Relative-Value Sleeve**: intrabar rejection imbalance, computed over 168 completed hourly bars: (upper wick minus lower wick)/(high-low), quote-volume weighted. Short the asset with strongest upper-wick rejection and long the asset with strongest lower-wick rejection, equal gross .5/.5; confidence clip(absolute cross-asset imbalance spread/.50,.25,1). This is not price residual, quote share, funding carry or beta.
4. **Stress/Recovery Sleeve**: in STRESS (>=3 assets negative net 168h AND median 24h high-low range >=1.5 times prior 168h median), long lowest 168h downside semivol; in RECOVERY (median 24h return positive AND >=2 assets with negative 168h return), long asset with largest rebound of close-location value from prior 168h low; in NORMAL, short .5 the asset with highest 168h downside semivol and long .5 lowest, unless tied. Confidence 1 STRESS/RECOVERY, .5 NORMAL.
5. **Regime Router**: the above STRESS/RECOVERY/NORMAL causal classifier. Gross targets NORMAL 1, STRESS .80, RECOVERY .95.
6. **Dynamic Allocator**: nominal budgets Core .55, Opportunity .20, RV .125, Stress .125; each optional multiplied by its precommitted confidence, unused optional budget goes to Core. **Execution-aware inertia:** each independently financed book uses .65 previous target + .35 newly calculated target (first epoch no inertia), then L1-normalizes to <=1; no hindsight turnover rescue. Removed sleeve budget flows to Core *before* inertia. All targets use only information through t.
7. **Risk Governor**: unchanged V98 generic prior-only order-invariant buffered authorization, physical next-open/mark settlement, signed funding, fees, slippage and solvency; no leverage or hidden borrowing.
