# V98 C16 — preregistered complete-system hypothesis (2026-11-18)

Status: **DESIGN ONLY; NOT EXECUTED; NO PROMOTION**. This is a new, independent V98 architectural hypothesis, not a retuning or rescue of rejected C14/C15. It must not access 2026+, the contaminated Phase083 holdout, XRP, or V14/V15/V99 implementation/results.

## Structural thesis
C14 persistence and C15 breakouts both failed the high-growth criterion under severe costs. C16 tests a *different* economic mechanism: slower risk-premium capture plus **cross-sectional dispersion harvesting with funding-aware position selection**, with tactical crash recovery. The portfolio is the experimental unit. Every signal is determined from completed bar t and executes no earlier than t+1 open.

## Seven slots (fixed before any result)
1. **Growth Core (45% budget)**: 30-day positive excess log-return versus equal-weight Clean-4 basket, conditioned on positive 90-day absolute return; allocate inverse 30-day realized volatility among eligible assets.
2. **Opportunity Sleeve (20%)**: 7-day return exceeding its own preceding 28-day median weekly return, *without* 14/90-day price-breakout conditions; positive 30-day trend required.
3. **Relative-Value Sleeve (20%)**: top two positive 30-day residual-return assets versus the contemporaneous equal-weight Clean-4 return; inverse-vol weighting. No shorts in initial C16 contract.
4. **Stress/Recovery Sleeve (15%)**: asset with 30-day drawdown at least 20%, followed by a completed 7-day return above 5% and positive 48-hour return. No future trough detection.
5. **Regime Router**: risk-on if 30-day equal-weight basket return positive and 30-day breadth >= 2 assets; otherwise half budget; zero if basket 30-day return below -20%.
6. **Dynamic Allocator**: proportional redistribution among *active* sleeves, preserving 45/20/20/15 relative budgets, with no redistribution when all sleeves inactive. Deterministic gross exposure cap 0.90 and single-asset cap 0.40; zero eligible assets means cash.
7. **Risk Governor**: completed-equity peak-to-trough throttle at >10% (0.5 multiplier), >20% (0.25), and full multiplier otherwise; reject invalid/nonfinite state.

Funding is charged from the authenticated native Binance USD-M funding stream, not an invented constant. Funding can be used only as a *cost* in this initial hypothesis, not as an optimized signal. Cost scenarios fixed at 1x, 2x, 4x. No leverage beyond gross 0.90; do not infer performance from gross returns.
