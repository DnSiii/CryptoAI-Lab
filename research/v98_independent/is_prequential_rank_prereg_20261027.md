## Seven slots and prequential allocation (one hypothesis, no grids)

At each epoch, define three consecutive completed nonoverlapping 168-hour blocks of x, B1 (t-503..t-336), B2 (t-335..t-168), B3 (t-167..t); R= B1+B2+B3. Let V_i = max(sqrt(mean(x[t-503:t]^2)), 0.0001), and cross-sectional ranks use deterministic stable ordering. Let E_i = (R_i - median_j R_j)/(V_i*sqrt(504)), and C_i = sum_{b=1}^3 1[B_b,i > median_j B_b,j]. Let market stress = median_j B3_j < 0, and recovery = median_j B3_j > 0 while median_j (B1_j+B2_j) < 0.
1. **Growth Core:** long-only normalized inverse volatility 1/V_i, with a **minimum BTC share 40%**: weights BTC = 0.4 + 0.6*(1/V_BTC)/sum(1/V), others 0.6*(1/V_i)/sum(1/V). No forecast lookahead.
2. **Opportunity Sleeve:** long 100% asset maximizing E_i among assets with C_i>=2 and R_i>0; otherwise CASH. Economic thesis: persistent relative strength across three independent weekly ranks.
3. **Relative-Value Sleeve:** if max C_i>=2 and min C_i<=1 and max(E)-min(E)>0.5, long 50% asset maximizing E_i, short 50% asset minimizing E_i; otherwise CASH. Economic thesis: self-financing cross-sectional rank separation rather than directional beta.
4. **Stress/Recovery Sleeve:** STRESS: short 100% asset with lowest B3_i/V_i; RECOVERY: long 100% asset with largest B3_i/V_i; NORMAL: CASH. Thesis: convex defensive/repair exposure only when broad cross-sectional regime is confirmed.
5. **Regime Router:** STRESS if median B3<0; RECOVERY if median B3>0 and median(B1+B2)<0; NORMAL otherwise.
