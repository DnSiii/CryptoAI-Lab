3. **Relative Value**: every 48h when idle, rank four assets by 336h native-funding-adjusted return **relative to the cross-sectional median**, penalized by 0.35 * downside RMS * sqrt(168). Long the top asset at 0.12 NAV only if its relative excess score >0.025 and its 336h absolute net return >0.
