## Seven slots: one immutable hypothesis
1. **Growth Core:** long-only weights proportional to positive 504h native-net cumulative log return divided by 504h downside semivol (floor .002); fallback inverse downside semivol when none positive. Minimum nominal core budget .50.
2. **Opportunity (capital-flow leadership):** at t compute each asset's latest 72h fraction of total native quote volume relative to its prior 336h fraction (t-407:t-72); multiply its latest 72h net log trend by that *relative quote-share change*. Long the maximum **positive** score; if none positive, long the asset with the largest signed score. No previous compression, beta, or spot signals.
3. **Relative Value (migration spread):** measure asset's log quote-share change between the latest 168h and preceding 168h, divided by its prior 336h quote-share dispersion floor .005; long top-ranked and short bottom-ranked with .5 gross each. Quote-share (not price residual or funding carry) is the proposed independent relative-value source.
4. **Stress/Recovery (conditional nonlinear response):** compute each asset's completed 24h return and its prior 168h downside semivol. In STRESS, long .5 gross the strongest 24h *risk-adjusted* return and short .5 gross the weakest (if tie, long only); in RECOVERY, long the highest 24h recovery from its prior 168h minimum close; in NORMAL, long the asset with largest 168h upside-semivol / downside-semivol ratio. No JG least-risk defensive asset selection.
5. **Regime Router:** STRESS when >=3/4 assets have negative 168h net returns AND equal-weight index drawdown from its 168h maximum >=8%; else RECOVERY when median 24h return >0 and >=2 assets have negative 168h return; otherwise NORMAL. Physical gross NORMAL 1.00, STRESS .80, RECOVERY .95.
6. **Dynamic Allocator:** nominal Core .50, Opportunity .20, RV .15, Stress .15. Each optional sleeve receives precommitted causal confidence multiplier: Opportunity clip(|quote-share shift of winner|/.20, .25, 1); RV clip((top minus bottom standardized quote-share shift)/2, .25, 1); Stress 1 in STRESS or RECOVERY, .50 in NORMAL. Transfer all unused optional budgets to Core; no PnL fitting, no hindsight, no synthetic zero-return cash. Full and three independently financed physical ablations use the same rules with removed optional budgets reallocated to Core. Normalize L1<=1.
7. **Risk Governor:** existing prior-only order-invariant physical V98 governor, next actual open/mark, signed funding and cash settlement, no borrowing; base/severe/supersevere fee/slippage, both funding orders.
