# V98 C22 — preregistered continuous participation architecture (training-only)

Recorded before any C22 replay or PnL observation. C18–C21 remain rejected; no parameter rescue. C22 uses only authenticated Clean-4, December 2022 warmup through December 2025. Never use XRP, 2026+, contaminated Phase083 holdout, or V14/V15/V99 information.

Hypothesis: C21's sparse binary breadth gate and 168-hour rebalancing leave too much cash during profitable but mixed regimes. A continuous long-only four-sleeve portfolio with daily targets and explicit trend quality should increase persistent participation without turning into unrestricted leverage. Distinct from C21: continuous trend strength, volatility-adjusted allocation, mean-reversion entry and breadth-sensitive gross participation rather than binary cross-sectional leader lists.

Fixed architecture before replay: Growth Core 50% (90-day positive trend + 30-day confirmation, continuous strength), Opportunity 15% (7-day pullback in positive 90-day trend), Relative Value 15% (positive 30-day relative excess and 90-day positive trend), Stress/Recovery 10% (7-day rebound after 30-day drawdown). Regime Router: breadth of 90-day positive trends, 0.5 at zero, 0.75 at one, 1.0 at two or more. Dynamic Allocator: 24-hour rebalance, sleeve allocations not redistributed when inactive, volatility-inverse scores. Risk Governor: existing causal equity governor, gross <= 0.90, asset <= 0.40. No shorts, leverage or implicit borrowing. Completed close t -> next open t+1.

Prespecified acceptance: independent full-ledger reconciliation and native funding, 27,048 hours, cost multipliers 1x/2x/4x, strictly positive compounded growth for all scenarios, positive annual returns in 2023, 2024 and 2025 at 4x; then separate chronological validation, concentration/tail/regime diagnostics and positive marginal contribution ablations before any internal freeze. Failure means rejection without retuning thresholds. This experiment cannot be declared validated by training alone.
