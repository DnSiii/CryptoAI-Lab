## Seven slots and fixed architecture
1. **Growth Core**: long-only weights proportional to positive 672h *net-of-native-funding* log return divided by 672h hourly volatility (floor .002), with inverse-volatility fallback. Normalize to L1=1.
2. **Opportunity Sleeve**: 48h momentum per unit volatility times clipped recent/prior quote participation; invest long-only in best positive-ranked asset (if all nonpositive, use strongest rank rather than hiding an unfinanced sleeve). Independent 48h versus 672h Core horizon.
3. **Relative-Value Sleeve**: pair trade long highest and short lowest *168h cross-asset funding-adjusted log-growth residual* (demeaned by cross-sectional median); half gross on each. This is not a JA forecast coefficient or an IX long-only alt rotation.
4. **Stress/Recovery Sleeve**: STRESS long asset with least 168h downside semideviation, RECOVERY long largest 48h return minus 168h drawdown penalty, NORMAL long lowest 168h downside semideviation. Always long-only.
5. **Regime Router**: STRESS when 168h median log growth <0 and average off-diagonal 168h return correlation >0.55; RECOVERY when 48h median log growth >0 and median 168h drawdown <-0.07; NORMAL otherwise. Frozen gross: NORMAL 1.00, STRESS .85, RECOVERY .95.
6. **Dynamic Allocator**: deterministic regime-conditioned online wealth-regret *continuous* expert mixture, not posterior expected-return covariance optimization. At epoch t update each expert score using only its **previous epoch t-168 portfolio** and realized 168h next-hour close-to-close net log returns with native funding through completed t; deduct fixed proxy 0.0008 per unit of its change from previous expert target. Log utility uses log(clip(1+expert dot net simple return, min=.05)) per hour. Score exponentially forgets with factor 0.85. Score is clipped to [-.15,+.15]; softmax temperature 3 over log prior [.70,.12,.10,.08] plus score; per-expert continuous floor .04, then normalize. The regime router changes exposure gross only, never selects an expert based on contemporaneous return. Four target portfolios are mixed into one shared-collateral asset vector; if netting lowers L1 gross, normalize to L1=1 (no leverage >1). Five independently financed counterfactual paths: Core-only, Full, minus each of Opportunity/RV/Stress. Every minus path renormalizes remaining ex-ante expert weights and recomputes the netted target; no synthetic cash or post-result refit.
7. **Risk Governor**: prior-only buffered physical exit/margin governor, native actual-next-open settlement, signed funding, base/severe/supersevere fee/slippage, both funding order conventions. USD10k cash-settled ledger with no hidden borrowing.
