2. **Opportunity:** persistent *price efficiency* trend: compute each asset's 168h absolute net log price change divided by the sum of its 168 absolute hourly log changes, multiply by the sign of its native-funding-net 168h drift, and take long 100% of the highest positive efficiency asset; if no positive efficiency, long highest 168h net drift. Always produces a physically financed directional asset, not an IA quote breakout.
3. **Relative Value:** causal beta-residual dispersion: regress each altcoin's previous 168 hourly log returns on BTC's previous 168 hourly log returns with zero intercept, clip beta to [0,3], then compute each alt's 72h return residual vs BTC; include BTC residual zero. Long 50% of highest residual and short 50% of lowest residual, stable ties. No HZ raw momentum ranking or IA funding-spread threshold.
4. **Stress/Recovery:** BTC directional regime exposure: short BTC 100% when BTC 72h funding-net drift < -0.05 or median Clean-4 72h funding-net drift < -0.035; otherwise long BTC 100%. This is a benchmark-relative convex crash hedge, not HZ short-weakest or IA conditional cash.
