1. **Growth Core**: every 168 completed hours, use long-only weights proportional to positive native-funding-net 504h trend divided by trailing 336h hourly realized volatility (floor 0.002), with a +0.025 floor per asset before normalization. No risk-parity objective; maximize trend participation.
2. **Opportunity**: one zero-sum candidate tilt (+0.20 strongest, -0.20 weakest) on 72h native-net **return acceleration** (net72 - net336*72/336), only when 168h market breadth >=2 and strongest net72>0.
3. **Relative Value**: one zero-sum candidate tilt (+0.16 weakest, -0.16 strongest) on *cross-sectional 168h residual* relative to the four-asset basket, only when residual spread >=0.025. This is mean reversion, unlike FD's risk-normalized momentum.
4. **Stress/Recovery**: one zero-sum candidate tilt (+0.18 BTC, -0.18 weakest 168h asset) when 168h basket native-net<0 and BTC is not weakest; otherwise (+0.18 strongest 24h, -0.18 weakest 168h) when basket 24h>0 and 168h basket<0.04, if distinct.

6. **Dynamic Allocator (novel causal online expert mixture)**: evaluate each candidate zero-sum sleeve's *hypothetical* prior 336h signed price-plus-native-funding contribution using only completed t data; subtract conservative friction 0.0015 times its L1 turnover. Convert the net prior evidence into a bounded participation factor 0.15 if nonpositive, 0.50 if positive, and 1.00 if above 0.012, **without fitting any coefficients on 2023–2024**. Combine core plus participation-scaled tilts, clip at zero, normalize to exactly one long-only book, and send one target on the 168h clock. Counterfactual expert evaluation is not an execution/PnL claim and cannot use future observations.
7. **Risk Governor**: unchanged CG buffered physical governor, 60% stressed NAV buffer, staged 81-corner exit, prior-only native liquidity/funding, base/severe/supersevere costs and both funding orders. No hidden leverage, short collateral or netting loophole.
