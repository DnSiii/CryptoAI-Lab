## Seven fixed slots, one funded physical book
Define x_i(s)=log(close_i(s)/close_i(s-1))-funding_i(s)*event_i(s), and n_i(w)=sum x_i over the most recent w completed hours. Use the last 336 hourly x for a sample covariance with fixed 0.25 diagonal shrinkage and a variance floor (0.0002)^2; all forecasts prior-only.
1. **Growth Core:** diversified long-only inverse covariance-risk contribution: invvol_i=1/sqrt(cov_ii); covariance penalty_i=1/(1+max(0,sum_j corr_ij)/4); weight_i proportional invvol_i*covariance_penalty_i, normalized. No momentum ranking. Economic mechanism: persistent compensated market exposure with reduced covariance concentration.
2. **Opportunity:** cross-horizon *acceleration*, not trend-efficiency or prior liquidity-impact: a_i=n_i(48)-(48/168)*n_i(168), scaled by 48h realized volatility. If max a_i > 0 and at least one n_i(168)>0, go 100% long eligible max a_i/(0.001+sqrt(sum_{48}x_i^2)); otherwise CASH.
3. **Relative Value:** 336-hour BTC-neutral residual dislocation. For each alt j, estimate beta_j=clip(cov(j,BTC)/cov(BTC,BTC),0.25,2) using prior 336 hourly net returns. Residual last-48h = n_j(48)-beta_j*n_BTC(48); standardized by sqrt(sum last336 (x_j-beta*x_BTC)^2)*sqrt(48/336). If any residual z < -0.25, select most negative z and hold long alt and short beta-weighted BTC with unit total absolute gross. Otherwise CASH. Economic mechanism: mean reversion of idiosyncratic downside overshoot, not funding-dispersion or trend ranking.
4. **Stress/Recovery:** covariance-contagion hedge. If median(n_i(72))<0, long 100% the asset with lowest beta to the equal-weight market over 336h. Otherwise long 100% the asset with highest beta to the equal-weight market. No short and no look-ahead. Economic mechanism: conditional beta exposure, not upside/downside semivol ranking.
5. **Regime Router:** STRESS if median(n72)<0 and mean off-diagonal correlation of 336h x exceeds 0.60; RECOVERY if median(n72)<0 but median(n24)>0; NORMAL otherwise.
6. **Dynamic Allocator:** fixed budgets Core/Opp/RV/Stress STRESS [.62,.08,.10,.20], RECOVERY [.50,.20,.15,.15], NORMAL [.55,.20,.15,.10]. Any inactive/ablated optional sleeve transfers its whole budget to Core. Precommitted gross STRESS 0.72, RECOVERY 0.94, NORMAL 0.90.
