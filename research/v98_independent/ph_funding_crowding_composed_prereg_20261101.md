4. **Stress/Recovery** (systemic deleveraging): If >=3 assets OI72<-0.04 and BTC r24<-0.04, short BTC -0.20 (cascade). Otherwise if >=3 assets OI72<-0.04, BTC r72<-0.08 and BTC r24>0.02, long BTC +0.20 (post-cascade reversal). Otherwise cash. Cascade has precedence.
