# V98 Independent DC — preregistered orthogonal inventory-partition growth engine (2026-10-25)

**Ex-ante complete seven-slot hypothesis.** DB is permanently rejected before PnL because its weekly shock Opportunity had only 3/2 effective epochs. DC is not a DB threshold rescue: it tests *cross-sectional growth with inventory-exclusive sleeves*, assigning assets before sending any target to the unchanged generic physical governor. It does not reuse DB/CZ/DA signals, parameters or selected budgets. Objective is maximal long-run net compounding subject to physical survival; 2025 holdout, 2026+ and V14/V15/V99 are forbidden in training.

Data: authenticated Clean-4 Binance USD-M BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL 1h Dec-2022 warmup through Dec-2024 training; native signed 8h funding/mark. XRP quarantined. Completed-close decisions t=1008+168k, first fill next actual open t+1.

1. Growth Core: on each weekly close, among all four assets, select the single highest positive 336h native-funding-adjusted log drift **only if** its latest 72h price drift is also positive; else cash. One long asset, not a multiasset average.
2. Opportunity: conditional secondary momentum. When BTC 168h drift positive and at least two assets have positive 48h drift, long the asset with highest positive 48h drift **excluding the selected core asset**, provided it is not used by a simultaneously selected stress position. Otherwise cash. This is not a rare 24h tail reversion.
3. Relative Value: cross-sectional native funding spread **carry**, not relative-price trend. Among assets unused by Growth and Opportunity at the same epoch, choose lowest and highest sums of the last 14 native 8h funding settlements; long lowest funding and short highest, each half gross, if at least two unused assets and a strictly positive spread. No price-based sign rescue.
4. Stress/Recovery: short BTC only when BTC 168h drift negative and >=3 assets have negative 48h drift; long BTC only on a 48h BTC sign reversal after prior negative 48h while BTC 168h still negative. Stress is eligible only if BTC is not occupied by Growth or Opportunity; otherwise cash.
5. Regime Router: RISK_ON if >=3 assets positive 336h adjusted drift and BTC 168h positive; RISK_OFF if <=1 and BTC 168h negative; otherwise MIXED.
6. Dynamic Allocator: weekly *inventory partition* (not DB post-authorization residual bisection). Compute Growth and Stress first, then Opportunity excluding their occupied assets, then Relative Value exclusively on remaining two or more assets. Precommitted budgets [Core,Opportunity,RV,Stress] RISK_ON [2.25,.35,.35,.0], MIXED [1.60,.40,.40,.35], RISK_OFF [.0,.15,.35,.80]. If regime makes budget zero, signal may still be computed but source gate counts only effective budget-positive epochs. Risk Governor may further reduce any target; no borrowing or hidden gross.
7. Risk Governor: unchanged generic buffered prior-only 81-corner/24h staged physical authorizer, actual-open settlement, 0.25% participation, native funding/mark and base/severe costs.

**Immutable source gate:** future-perturbation invariance, exact pinned source SHA, 2023 and 2024 effective epochs >=4 Growth/Opportunity/Stress and >=8 RV. Fail => reject without PnL.
