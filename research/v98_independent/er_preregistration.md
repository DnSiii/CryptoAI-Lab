# V98 Independent ER — preregistered prequential convex-growth composition (2026-10-25)
**Pre-result, immutable seven-slot hypothesis.** ER is an independently designed *online conditional-compounding* system, not a parameter adjustment or rescue of rejected EQ, EP or EO. It tests whether **causally scored recent sleeve persistence** and separate price-efficiency, residual-strength and recovery mechanisms can add positive *annual marginal net log wealth* to a slow diversified growth core. All design choices are fixed before the authenticated historical economic run. Rejected candidate signals, weights and returns were not reused as ER selection inputs.

## Complete seven-slot portfolio, 48-hour observation epochs
1. Growth Core: 504h funding-adjusted positive trend, 168h positive confirmation, 336h downside-semivolatility normalization, 15% equal-floor / 85% normalized winners; equal fallback.
2. Opportunity: **price-efficiency/participation** long-only strongest alt with positive 96h net momentum, close in top 25% of its *prior 168h completed high/low range*, and latest 24h quote turnover above prior 168h median of 24h turnover. Flat otherwise. No lookahead range.
3. Relative Value: **cross-sectional residual continuation**, long-only asset with highest positive 168h funding-adjusted return minus cross-sectional median, subject to positive 72h net momentum and 168h native funding no worse than cross-sectional median. Flat otherwise; no paired short.
4. Stress/Recovery: **broad-reversal convex participation**, long-only strongest 24h winner when equal-market 168h price return is negative, 24h equal-market return positive and >=2 assets have positive 24h return. Flat otherwise.
5. Regime Router: TREND if >=3 assets have positive 504h net trend, STRESS if <=1, MIXED otherwise.
6. Dynamic Allocator: Growth Core gross demand 2.25 TREND, 2.0 MIXED, 1.65 STRESS. Base sleeve gross requests .13/.12/.15 respectively. For each sleeve, at completed-close t, its last up to 12 *completed* 48h shadow signal episodes are scored on their realized native-funding-adjusted log returns minus 4bp estimated round-trip friction. The multiplier is exp(5 x mean episode net return), clipped [.50,1.40], with neutral 1.0 before evidence; never uses contemporaneous future outcome or any validation period. This is an online **prequential allocation rule**, not fitted from results. Shared gross cap 2.8, core-first; missing sleeves receive zero, no hidden borrow or synthetic free capital.
7. Risk Governor: unchanged CG 60% stressed NAV reserve, 81-corner staged exit and prior-only liquidity/funding, native actual-open settlement, both funding orderings, base/severe/supersevere fees/slippage. No voluntary rebalancing between epochs; forced physical exits remain mandatory.
