## Independent economic hypothesis
Persistent realized funding polarity is a directly observable carry transfer. A position aligned with both trailing price direction and the side **receiving** funding may compound more reliably than pure price trend. Cross-asset funding spread can create dollar-neutral carry exposure; a sudden price dislocation with stale crowded funding may create stress convexity. These are not PK premium-level reversal or standardized premium dislocation. The authenticated premium-index CSV is used only as a **same-sign sanity check on funding direction**, not a PK z-score; premium bar t is not closed at decision t and cannot be used.
