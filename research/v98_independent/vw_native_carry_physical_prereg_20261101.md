## Fixed cash/hedge mechanics
Starting equity USD 10,000. Each first-of-month UTC 00:00 rebalance closes existing spot/perp and opens four 1:1 unit-matched spot-long/perp-short positions. For each symbol q=0.10*preopen_equity/max(spot_open,perp_open), no leverage, no rebalance within month. Spot buy/sell cashflows and perpetual short entry/exit realized variation, explicit fees/slippage and native signed funding go to one cash account. Perpetual unrealized short PnL marked hourly; spot inventory marked hourly; equity=cash+spot_marked_value+perp_unrealized. No mark-to-market double counting. Baseline per-fill fee+slippage in bps: spot 10+2, perp 5+2; severe spot 20+5, perp 15+5; supersevere spot 30+10, perp 25+10. These are *illustrative* not verified account-specific executable costs. At every hourly bar, enforce adverse simultaneous spot low/perp high equity above 5% of short notional and free cash above 15% of short notional; reject if violated, never silently borrow or liquidate at invented prices. Funding on held units * conservative event mark * signed rate. All 4 assets are held, never choose BTC/ETH after seeing funding outcomes. No hidden leverage or new alpha signals.
