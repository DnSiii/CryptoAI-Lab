## Economic hypothesis (genuinely different architecture)
Previous static composed systems routinely had optional sleeves with negative/zero full-minus-ablation marginal log growth. PF tests **continuous, economically independent sleeves with causal realized net-contribution budget competition**, rather than preselected static sleeve priorities or another cosmetic core reweight. The allocator does not optimize using realized 2022–2025 PF results. At each decision it uses only earlier completed weeks of *its own* hypothetical sleeves, including predeclared cost/funding estimates. Hypothesis: this feedback can preserve core growth while deploying scarce capital to genuinely complementary sleeves, with opportunity/RV/stress each adding robust positive marginal net log growth to the complete portfolio.

- Weekly decisions at hour `t=744,912,...` (157 total), using data through the completed candle `t`; physical fills at `t+1` open, causal t-1 decision execution. No unbounded leverage: virtual sleeve gross <=0.84 NAV, optional total <=0.34, core <=0.50; same native base/severe/supersevere fee/slippage, both funding orders, hourly pretrade/intrabar/posttrade solvency checks.
- **Growth Core:** 28d log-momentum ranked by 14d realized-vol-normalized 28d momentum; when BTC 28d momentum positive, long top two positive-ranked assets at +0.30/+0.20; when negative, short bottom two negative-ranked assets at -0.30/-0.20; otherwise cash. No cross-sleeve parameters.
- **Opportunity Sleeve:** realized range compression (last 7d mean (H-L)/C below 70% of prior 21d) followed by 48h close-to-close expansion; long largest positive 48h return if >+3%, short most negative if <-3%; target signed 0.20, otherwise cash. Deterministic asset-index ties.
- **Relative-Value Sleeve:** 28d OLS BTC-beta residual in 7d log return, select largest absolute alt residual; enter opposite the residual at 0.16 on alt and beta-neutral opposite hedge on BTC at `-alt_weight * clip(beta,0,1.5)`; require abs residual >=4%; otherwise cash.
- **Stress/Recovery Sleeve:** if >=3 assets have 7d log return below -7%, short the weakest 48h asset at -0.20; if >=3 assets have 7d return below -7% but 48h breadth >=3 positive, instead long BTC +0.20. Recovery takes precedence over stress. Otherwise cash.
- **Regime Router:** stressed when current BTC 7d realized variance >1.5 times previous 21d BTC realized variance; otherwise normal. Stress regime prioritizes stress sleeve in equal causal-score ties; normal regime prioritizes opportunity then RV then stress. This is tie-breaking only.
- **Dynamic Allocator:** at each weekly decision, for each optional sleeve independently compute the mean of up to the **last 8 completed weekly hypothetical net cash returns** from prior raw sleeve targets (from their original decision, never current prices); return = previous raw weights dot close-to-close asset returns, minus `2 * (0.001 + 0.001) * gross(raw)` (conservative assumed entry+exit fee/slip) and minus signed native funding rates over the completed week weighted by previous raw weights. This estimator is **not** system PnL and not a substitute for physical accounting. An optional sleeve is eligible only if it has at least 4 completed observations and mean net return >0; rank eligible sleeves by mean net return descending (regime tie order). Fill optional gross budget 0.34 in that order, each at most its predeclared raw gross. Unused budget remains cash; no allocation to negative estimated contributors. Core retains its independent predeclared raw exposure.
- **Risk Governor:** enforce <=0.84 total virtual gross, <=0.34 optional gross, <=0.50 core gross, no cash overdraft/no hidden leverage; use existing atomic physical executor and stress/forced safety fills. Zero-volume and native-funding events fail closed.
