## Economic distinction and falsifiable mechanism
PG does **not** trade simple OI growth in the same direction as recent price (OD/OJ), price-minus-OI rotation, raw funding carry, or realized sleeve-PnL ranking (PF). It tests **inventory stock-to-flow innovation before price displacement**: if outstanding positions grow relative to transacted quote-volume *while price remains compressed*, accumulated inventory can precede directional repricing; differences in that inventory build across assets can generate a spread without already-established price divergence. Both claims can fail economically. A covariance-aware allocator charges overlapping capital rather than using past candidate PnL to rank sleeves. No parameter tuning after observed PG results.
