CE tests whether persistent BTC/ETH **market-premium exposure**, plus sparse native funding carry and asset rotation, yields higher long-run compounded wealth than repeated short-lived technical signals. This is structurally distinct from CD's 2160h/720h gated top-three momentum, CD's level-z-score RV, CC's residual mean reversion and rejected CB/CA/BZ/BY signals. No rejected engine parameters, signals, results or benchmark inputs are imported. V98-only physical settlement and source verification may be reused as execution plumbing only.

1. **Growth Core**: persistent fixed **BTC 65% / ETH 35%** long market-premium inventory. Authorization only every **2016h** (12 weeks), never directional timing or backtested ranking; physical governor may force earlier reduction but no hidden re-risk. No moving average or price-momentum filter.
2. **Opportunity Sleeve**: every **672h**, consider BNB and SOL only. If 672h log return exceeds the contemporaneous BTC/ETH weighted basket return by >0.08 and the candidate's observed trailing 672h native funding sum is <= BTC/ETH weighted funding sum, long the higher excess; else cash. Hold 672h. Genuine alt-relative rotation, not CD high-breakout or CC acceleration.
3. **Relative-Value Sleeve**: every **2016h**, rank assets by trailing 2016h signed funding paid by a long. Long the minimum-funding asset and short the maximum-funding asset (equal gross 50/50), only when the historical *funding differential* >0.035 over 2016h; else cash. Hypothesis is priced funding carry, not level/return spread mean reversion. Native realized funding charged at each future event; trailing funding is not assumed to predict future funding.
4. **Stress/Recovery Sleeve**: every **336h**, short BTC only when BTC has fallen >25% from its maximum completed close in the last 672h AND 336h realized downside hourly RMS exceeds 1344h realized downside RMS; else cash. No unconditional stress overlay, no CD's 18%/6% dual trigger.
5. **Regime Router**: CRISIS if BTC 672h close-to-peak drawdown >25%; BULL if BTC last 1344h log return >0 and >=3 assets last 672h log returns >0; otherwise RANGE.
6. **Dynamic Allocator**: fixed per-regime gross target budgets [Core,Opportunity,RV,Stress]: BULL [1.60,.55,.25,0.], RANGE [1.25,.35,.30,.25], CRISIS [.65,0.,.30,.60]. Budget frozen at each module's own update. No performance-driven parameter changes.
7. **Risk Governor**: original V98-only BAGovernor with 25% prior 24h staged exit capacity, 0.25% prior-hour participation, 24h adverse continuation, signed native funding reserves, physical no-instant-exit ratchet, original three fee/slippage tiers and both funding orderings, actual-open fills, 81-corner intrabar solvency.
