# V98 PQ — asymmetric downside-risk transmission, preregistered before economic inspection (2026-11-01)

**Hypothesis:** downside-versus-upside realized semivariance (not simple volatility magnitude, trend, premium z-score, funding polarity, OI or BTC lead-lag) identifies the assets that can compound after market-wide forced deleveraging. Growth follows positive risk-adjusted 21-day direction only when downside asymmetry is contained; the other three sleeves trade distinct conditional liquidity and skew mechanisms. A failed PQ is permanently rejected. This preregistration is binding before PQ implementation and any exposed-history PnL.

**Universe and clock:** V98-only SHA-checked Clean-4 BTC/ETH/BNB/SOL USD-M 1h, Dec-2022 warmup to Dec-2025 exposed training. 157 weekly decisions at t=744+168k, fills at t+1 open. No 2026+, XRP, Phase083, external benchmarks, prospective holdout or paper. Use OHLC close and quote volume only through t; 504 preceding hourly log returns for downside/upside semivariance. Funding is native historical rates, worst-side 1h mark envelopes, not exact funding-event marks. Never use t+1 information to decide.

**Frozen feature definitions:** r24/r72/r168/r504 = log(C_t/C_{t-k}); downside semi-RMS = sqrt(mean(min(hourly_log_return,0)^2)) and upside semi-RMS = sqrt(mean(max(hourly_log_return,0)^2)) on 504 completed hourly returns; asymmetry A=(downside+.0001)/(upside+.0001). Volume expansion V = sum(q[t-71:t+1]) / max(sum(q[t-143:t-71]),1), clipped at 4. Vol504 = std(hourly log returns)*sqrt(504), floor .04. Ties by fixed Clean-4 index. No post-PnL parameter adjustments.
