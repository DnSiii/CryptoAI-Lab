## Seven slots — exact precommitted deterministic algorithm
1. **Growth Core** (long-only): every 168 completed hours, for each asset compute native-net log close return over 672h and 336h (signed funding summed over that window), and 336h hourly close-log-return std floored at .002. Score = max(0, .65*net672+.35*net336)/vol336 + .04; normalize scores to sum one. This is a two-horizon persistence ensemble rather than GH's 672h-only core.
2. **Opportunity Sleeve — volume-weighted directional participation efficiency**: over last 72 completed hours, each bar's signed body fraction = clip((close-open)/(high-low),-1,+1), zero-range=0; compute sum(signed_body*quote_volume)/max(sum(quote_volume),1e-9). Eligible asset has efficiency>+.05 and native-net72>0 and native-net168>0. Choose eligible highest net72/vol336, subtract from weakest net168 (distinct); zero-sum +.18/- .18; fixed allocator coefficient .55. This measures directional participation rather than GH's 24h CLV/volume breakout.
3. **Relative Value Sleeve — funding dispersion**: 168h signed funding event sum for each asset; if cross-asset max-min>0.0005, long the lowest funding burden and short the highest via zero-sum +.14/- .14 score; allocator coefficient .60. Reusing a distinct, pre-existing positively evidenced first-party source family does NOT rehabilitate GH's rejected complete engine.
4. **Stress/Recovery Sleeve — volatility-expansion recovery quality**: compute mean per-hour high-low/open range over last 24h divided by mean range over last 168h, then cross-asset median. When ratio>1.15, calculate 168h hourly downside semivol sqrt(mean(min(hourly_log_return,0)^2)); select the least downside-semivol asset as receiver, and most downside-semivol asset as donor. If basket native-net24>0, receiver instead is argmax(net72/max(downside_semivol,.002)); donor remains highest downside semivol. Distinct receiver/donor => zero-sum +.18/- .18; coefficient .65. In inactive states all zeros. This tests realized volatility-expansion quality/recovery, not GH intrabar-low shock.
5. **Regime Router**: based on 336h native-net breadth and mean 24h native-net, EXPANSION when >=3 positive and basket24>0, DEFENSE when <=1 positive, else ROTATION. Gross demand 2.30/1.25/1.85, respectively; no implicit leverage or borrowing.
6. **Dynamic Allocator**: single self-financed book, raw score = core + .55*opportunity + .60*relative_value + .65*stress; clip at zero and normalize to sum one; full and three leave-one-out books independently normalize. No retrospective weighting or separate collateral.
7. **Risk Governor**: same independent CG 60% stressed-NAV buffer, 81-corner staged exit, prior-only conservative quote liquidity, native funding, base/severe/supersevere costs and both funding orderings. No discretionary exceptions.
