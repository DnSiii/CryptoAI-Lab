## Seven fixed slots, no fitting
1. **Growth Core:** every 168h, rank 504h funding-net log-return divided by 336h downside semivol floor .002; long top three 50/30/20. No short positions.
2. **Opportunity:** price-range compression-to-breakout ranking. At each completed epoch, for each asset compute normalized distance of current close above its prior 72h high (exclude current hour) divided by prior 168h realized hourly standard deviation (floor .002). Multiply by ratio of prior 336h median hourly high-low log-range to prior 48h median range, clipped [0.5,2]. Long the highest score; score can be negative (near-breakout leader). This tests breakout proximity under range contraction rather than order-flow share.
3. **Relative Value:** among the three highest prior 336h quote-turnover assets, long lowest prior 336h signed funding burden. Unchanged native source; evaluated as independent marginal sleeve.
4. **Stress/Recovery:** when broad median 72h funding-net return < -0.06 OR broad median 336h funding-net return < -0.12, **hold this sleeve in USD cash** (zero crypto units), preserving optionality without reducing Core/Opportunity/RV budgets. Outside stress, long the highest 24h funding-net return / 168h downside semivol floor .002 as recovery/continuation. This cash-vs-asset decision is explicit and causal.
5. **Regime Router:** STRESS if (4) cash trigger, TREND if >=3 assets have positive 504h funding-net returns, TRANSITION otherwise. Gross TREND .74, TRANSITION .72, STRESS .66.
6. **Dynamic Allocator:** deterministic weights TREND [.55,.25,.12,.08], TRANSITION [.58,.17,.15,.10], STRESS [.55,.10,.10,.25]. When stress cash sleeve is included, it reserves its budget in cash; other sleeves retain their relative budgets and there is **no re-normalization to 100%**. If an optional sleeve is independently ablated, its full budget finances Core; for Stress/Recovery ablation in STRESS, cash is replaced by funded Core. Every book is independently physically settled from USD10k.
7. **Risk Governor:** exact existing GL order-invariant buffered physical governor, native funding/mark, adverse liquidation 81-corner and exit reserve. No relaxation.
