## One non-optimizable decision rule
The numeric evaluator accepts two complete *independently reconstructed* portfolio daily USD-equivalent equity trajectories for identical 661 TRAIN dates, scenarios `severe` and `supersevere`, initial USD capital >0, and a same-clock original benchmark equity series. Daily USD NAV must be calculated upstream from original BTC/ETH inverse-coin cash, open liabilities, as-of underlying index, settlement, native fees, margin and liquidation, not interpolated marks. A separate provenance gate and independent journal replay are mandatory before a numeric pass can ever be treated as a real economic gate. This numeric module itself can never certify original data, validate a source, open sealed data, authorize promotion or trades.

Exactly 661 UTC end-of-day observations from 2021-12-09 through 2023-09-30 (23:59:59 UTC) are required in each scenario, with strictly positive equity and benchmark, identical clocks, all five **fixed** chronological folds from `research.tools.v99_r106_d1_real_train_source_join.FOLD_EDGES`, and all six 3x2 regime names `BULL/BEAR/SIDEWAYS` × `LOW/HIGH_VOL`. Regimes are upstream t-1 and must be independently verified; this evaluator never infers them from the return being scored. Every row has a canonical `actual_exposure_key` to report scenario-dependent path divergence, not to prefer the more flattering cost path.

For **both** severe and supersevere, require total compounded ROI >0, daily positive/negative profit factor >1, and positive compounded ROI after dropping the single highest daily log-return factor. Severe requires >=4/5 positive chronological fold returns; supersevere >=3/5. Both require max drawdown no worse than -35%, top ceil(1% of 661) positive daily **dollar gains** <=40% of all positive gains, and worst ceil(1% of 661) daily **dollar losses** <=50% of all losses. Require both scenarios' total ROI strictly above the matched original benchmark total ROI. Require each of the six regimes to appear at least once; per-regime returns are reported, not tuned or cherry-picked. These conservative numeric cutoffs are **research screening**, not proof of superiority to the legacy champion or live profitability.

No false `PASS` on missing days, nonfinite/zero capital, duplicate/extra days, missing regimes, reordered rows, missing benchmark, mismatched initial capital, wrong fold or sealed date, negative fees hidden in the upstream ledger, or single-scenario input. Any numeric pass is `NUMERIC_ONLY_PENDING_SOURCE_AND_INDEPENDENT_REPLAY`, with `promotion_authorized=false`, `freeze_authorized=false`, `validation_opened=false`, `holdout_opened=false`, `orders_enabled=false`.
