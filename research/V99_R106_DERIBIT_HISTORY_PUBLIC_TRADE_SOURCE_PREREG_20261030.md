- Two currencies BTC and ETH; preselected target UTC 2022-01-23 00:00–23:59:59.999, and source-alive control UTC 2022-05-01 (both within TRAIN [2021-12-09,2023-10-01)). Exactly four bounded requests, each at most three transport retries with delay; no scan across dates/instruments, no access to 2023-10-01 or later.
- Store each original response byte stream only as ephemeral GitHub Actions runner input for a separately implemented independent parser. Publish only SHA256, status, count, earliest/latest returned timestamp, and count of instrument names that parse as dated option contracts. Do not publish raw trade rows in the artifact or research dashboard.
- Verify HTTP 200, JSON-RPC `result.trades` list, nonnegative timestamps inside requested UTC window, BTC/ETH instrument prefix and dated put/call suffix, positive finite price/amount, and duplicate trade IDs where provided. If rate limited, network blocked, malformed, or empty: status `UNKNOWN_OR_EMPTY`, NOT historical absence; do not infer lack of liquidity.
- Even 100 valid trade prints are **not complete day coverage** and are **not executable quotes**. No native fee tier, bid/ask depth, option chain as-of, settled lifecycle, fill, 5-fold economics or 180-lifecycle certification can be derived from this probe.
- Independent verifier must reparse raw JSON bytes, independently verify sha and timestamps, enforce no permission flags and compare all reported counts. The raw byte files are deleted after verification and excluded from uploaded artifacts.
