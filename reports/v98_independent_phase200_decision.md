Status: **REJECT_FAMILY_NO_RESCUE**

## Evidence harvested

Phase200 completed successfully and reproducibly on the frozen training-only sample (2023/2024/2025 folds; no validation/final holdout access). All 8 preregistered volatility-compression breakout specifications failed the training gate.

Representative/best-return neighborhood remains economically unacceptable after realistic frictions: the family shows negative aggregate return and PF below 1, with large drawdowns; severe and supersevere cost scenarios deteriorate further. No post-hoc inversion, threshold rescue, or parameter expansion is permitted.

## Failure-mechanism audit

The failure is broad rather than attributable to one isolated tail event: performance is not rescued across the preregistered compression/breakout/holding combinations, and cost stress worsens an already non-viable gross mechanism. This is consistent with false/short-lived breakouts plus turnover drag rather than a stable continuation premium.

The small five-asset universe also makes cross-sectional quantile refinements inappropriate; Phase200 is therefore closed rather than tuned.

## Scientific decision

Reject Phase200 as a family. Do not open validation or final holdout. Do not use V99 evidence for selection. Preserve the result as negative evidence.

Next research direction must be scientifically distinct and preregistered before execution.
