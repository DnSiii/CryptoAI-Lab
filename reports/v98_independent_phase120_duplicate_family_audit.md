# V98 Independent — Phase120 duplicate-family audit

Status: CLOSED — NO PNL AUTHORIZED.

Phase119 passed its DATA_ONLY integrity gate, but an independent registry audit found that the proposed follow-on stablecoin-supply mechanism is not scientifically new. Phase060/061 already used the same DefiLlama `stablecoincharts/all` aggregate stablecoin-supply family, with a preregistered liquidity-expansion/contraction trading hypothesis.

Therefore Phase119 remains valid as a reproducible DATA_ONLY acquisition result, but it does **not** authorize another stablecoin-supply PnL experiment. Re-testing a nearby lookback, threshold, direction, basket, lag, smoothing rule, or exposure after the historical Phase061 evidence would create avoidable researcher degrees of freedom and violate the current anti-overfit standard.

Decision: `CLOSE_DUPLICATE_FAMILY_NO_PNL`. No Phase120 trading candidate is created, no validation/final holdout is opened, and no V16/V99 evidence is used. The next experiment must use a genuinely distinct external information family.
